How this works

Roles. Give each main or side deck card a role. A Starter can get your play going alone. Combo A and Combo B are pieces that only work together. An Extender needs a starter first. A Handtrap is interaction you want in hand. A Garnet is a card you never want to draw.

Fast assigning. Pick a role in the legend to use it as a brush, then click cards to paint them. With no brush selected, clicking a card cycles through the roles.

HOPT. If a card is hard once per turn, a second copy in the same hand is dead: it doesn't count as a starter, extender or combo piece.

Playable and brick. A hand is playable with at least one starter, or when any combo line matches. It's a brick when it isn't playable, or when it holds as many garnets as your brick limit.

Simulation. The calculator shuffles your main deck and draws a hand for every simulated trial. The random seed is fixed so the same deck always shows the same numbers. Press the reroll button to see how much the result moves.

Saving. Your deck, roles and combos save automatically in this browser. Roles are remembered per card, so cards you already tagged keep their role in other decks. Use "Copy share link" to send your deck with roles and combos to a friend.
